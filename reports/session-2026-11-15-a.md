## 1. Stuck bots

**Cause.** The still cluster was all eight hunters of a match robbing each other: a hunter
chased every opponent carrier, the other hunters too, and because a steal rounds up a single
coin always changed hands, so the loot circulated inside the pack, no hunter ever reached
`hunterSecureAt`, and inside `arriveRadius` none of them moved (seed 1: one bot for 215.6 s).
**Fix.** A hunter only chases a carrier richer than itself, walks right onto it, and steals
only when the carrier the core would rob (the nearest in range) is richer too.

Reproduction log (scratch script, seeded 32-bot matches, every bot standing still > 3 s
outside a secure): seeds 1–10 before the fix had 8, 29, 9, 19, 8, 12, 8, 11, 8, 23 episodes,
each a hunter with intent "steal", no direction, 0–3 coins, surrounded by other hunters with
0–3 coins. After the first half of the fix (prey must be richer) 0–5 per seed remained:
hunters beside a carrier securing at its shrine, because the core robs the nearest carrier in
range — a poorer hunter next to them — not the one they chased. After the full fix: seeds
1–20, 0 episodes. Test: `packages/game-core/src/bots/hunter.test.ts` (fails on the old code).
