## Piece 1 — the capture

`pnpm review:capture [game …]` (package `scripts/review-capture`, its own stack on 3936 / 5936 /
5937, seed 7, every mode in the rotation, the dev admin endpoint for its viewer). For each of
the four startable games — Sumo · Points, Sumo · Last One Standing, King of the Season, Halving
Bomb — it opens the viewer (1920 × 1080) and two clients, starts the game through the viewer's
"Next match" flow (choose, values shown, start) and follows one full match into the next one's
first seconds, twice: the scripted player on the desktop (1280 × 800) with a phone spectator
and the viewer shown, then the player on the phone (390 × 844, touch) with a desktop spectator.
31 bots and the player: it steers, dashes at whoever is close and jumps now and then (Sumo:
circles in the arena and can fall; King: makes for the zone; Bomb: makes for the nearest
character, to take or pass a bomb). Every moment is shot in Light and Dark (the page's own
switch, back and forth); every shot is measured (smallest font, texts cut at the edge, texts
overlapping, the lowest DOM text contrast, the field on screen, the body size) into
`metrics.jsonl`.

Moments: start screen; the admin flow (choose, started) and the panel folded; the first seconds;
help open; sound off; per halving 2 s before, its banner (+0.3 s), the island breaking or the
walls moving (+1.3 s) and the new season (+2.5 s); last chance; match over; result (+3 s) and
later (+8 s); the next match's first seconds. The player's own: fall and respawn (Sumo), out
and watching (Last One Standing), holding the zone (King), carrying, passing and the "no
return" mark (Bomb) — each when it first happens. There is no waiting or countdown screen
before a match: a started game begins at once (noted, not a fault of the capture).

Files: `reports/screens/review/<game>/<size>-<look>-<role>-<nn>-<moment>.png` (not committed),
contact sheets `reports/screens/review/sheet-<game>-<size>[-k].png`, sizes `desktop`, `phone`
and `big` (the viewer).
