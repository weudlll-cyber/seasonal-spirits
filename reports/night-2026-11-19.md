## Bomb touch

Measured only, nothing changed (piece 7). Halving Bomb on an in-process server (seeds 1–10, the
starting settings, 32 seats, one of them the scripted player), N = 10 matches per latency. The
scripted player is a WebSocket client that draws as the browser does: the others interpolated
50 ms behind the newest state (RenderClock, `interpolate`), its own character at the newest state
(where the prediction keeps it); it reacts to what it drew 250 ms before, and while it carries a
bomb it steers at the nearest takeable opponent on its picture (other team, no bomb, not in the
air, not falling) and dashes when it is within 4 body radii. Latency one way each direction:
LAN as is (0 ms added) and +80 ms. The pass reach is 34 field units (2 body radii + 2, game-core
`modes/bomb.ts`). The script stayed in the session's scratch folder.

**Per attempt** (one carry of a bomb, from getting it to losing it):

| Latency | Attempts N | Contact seen, passed | Seen, not passed | Not seen, passed | Not seen, not passed |
| ------- | ---------- | -------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | -------------------- |
| LAN     | 87         | 36                   | 32               | 11               | 8                    |
| +80 ms  | 81         | 35                   | 18               | 16               | 12                   |

("Not passed" ends at the blast of a halving or the match end.)

**Per seen contact** (a target first drawn within the reach while carrying): passed within
500 ms — LAN 19 of N = 201, +80 ms 24 of N = 178. For each seen contact without a pass, the
server's distance in the newest state at that moment and the target's motion:

| Latency | Seen, no pass N | Server distance median | min–max     | Within the reach | Target fleeing |
| ------- | --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| LAN     | 182             | 41.2                   | 31.1–68.2   | 47               | 154            |
| +80 ms  | 154             | 39.9                   | 31.1–66.1   | 55               | 120            |

Reading: most drawn contacts are not real at that moment — the target, drawn about 50 ms (LAN)
or 210 ms (+80) in the past, has already moved away (fleeing in 85 % and 78 %), so the server
measures it 6–7 units past the reach. 47 and 55 contacts were within the reach on the server too
and still no pass came within 500 ms: the server's other conditions (the target in the air, just
gave that bomb, already carrying one) were not logged — a next measurement would log them. About
a third of the carries with a seen contact end without a pass on the LAN, a quarter at +80 ms.
