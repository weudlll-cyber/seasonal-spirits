## Piece 3 — every gameplay value a setting

**The schema.** One list in game-core (`settings.ts`, `settings-list.ts`) holds every number that
shapes play — 83 settings with label, unit, range, step, default, the modes they belong to and
whether they apply now or from the next match. The game's config is built from it; the server
checks every value with it, the clients read the settings in force with it, the panel and the
docs table (`docs/SETTINGS.md`, `pnpm settings:doc`) are generated from it. A test fails when a
number of a mode's config comes from no setting, when game-core holds another numeric constant
than the eight listed as not gameplay (with their reasons), and when the docs table and the
schema part. Numbers that were constants in code are settings now: how much a halving shrinks
the arena and the zone, points per second in King and Bomb, the share a blast takes, the respawn
band, four bot brain shapes. The defaults are today's values (the halving factor 0.71 instead of
√½ = 0.7071, on its 0.01 step).

**The groups.** General (seats per team, ghost size, walking, the dash, the push, the jump,
respawn, protection, credit, countdown, result pause …), Bots (reaction, aim noise, form, dash
chance and gap, hesitation, jump chance, dash range and angle, the endgame's lead, boldness and
commit), Sumo (match and season length, arena size, halving shrink, lives, variant, after the
last season, keep-halving interval, the bots' edge margin), King of the Season (match length,
jump interval, field, zone size and shrink, points per second, bounce window, the bots' spot),
Halving Bomb (match length, blast interval, field, countdown, players per bomb, no pass back,
the blast's share, points per second, the bots' fleeing), Rotation (six match slots: off, Sumo,
Sumo Points, Sumo Last One Standing, King of the Season, Halving Bomb).

**How presets work.** In the viewer's panel: type a name, Save — the current settings are stored
as `data/presets/<name>.json` (git-ignored); pick one, Load — it is checked against the schema
and applied (now-values at once, the rest from the next match); Delete removes it. A preset with
a bad or unknown value is refused, never applied. `pnpm dev` starts with the last loaded preset;
`PRESET=<name>` names one. Presets replaced the copy line.

**The admin area (ADDENDUM).** The panel talks to one endpoint, `/api/v1/admin/settings`, with
one access check in front (`adminAccess`: today the dev flag, loopback and the viewer's page) —
built so that the game's own admin login takes that place later, as Seasonal Bingo Royale and
Seasonal Mini Games have it (read read-only; BACKLOG OPEN "af" names the files). CONCEPT.md
section 16 records that the new game runs standalone.

**Checked live.** 6 groups, 83 controls; the walking speed slider reached the server; a preset
saved, loaded back after a change, deleted; Reset per group; a foreign page 403. Screenshots
`reports/screens/latest/panel-desktop.png`, `panel-phone.png`.

### Own ghost stutter

Owner, playing: running straight at full speed, the own ghost sometimes does not move evenly.

**a) Prediction** — a scripted player through the client's real frame path (the state buffer,
`interpolate`, the render clock and `OwnPrediction` exactly as the client's `main.ts` drives
them), 60 s running straight back and forth across the field, at full speed, against a Sumo
server (9 lives) and a Bomb server, both reached by the machine's LAN address. "Straight": the
server reports the top speed, the direction held for over 0.5 s, not frozen. "Uneven": drawn
speed more than 20 % off the running speed. "Near": another ghost within three ghost radii.

| Run          | Straight frames | Uneven      | Near: uneven | Far: uneven | Correction p50 / p95 / max | Drawn speed σ |
| ------------ | --------------- | ----------- | ------------ | ----------- | -------------------------- | ------------- |
| Sumo before  | 1017            | 495 (49 %)  | 348 of 579   | 61 of 438   | 9.3 / 37.3 / 58.2 u        | 283 u/s       |
| Sumo after   | 431             | 128 (30 %)  | 125 of 243   | 14 of 188   | 5.8 / 24.1 / 46.0 u        | 198 u/s       |
| Bomb before  | 1986            | 151 (7.6 %) | 106 of 316   | 60 of 1670  | 1.8 / 19.7 / 40.3 u        | 87 u/s        |
| Bomb after   | 2400            | 101 (4.2 %) | 94 of 223    | 14 of 2177  | 1.4 / 15.2 / 41.7 u        | 30 u/s        |

(Correction: the gap the client's correction sees at each server state; N 893, 376, 1031, 1198.)
Before the fix the prediction's path ran up to 169 u away from the server and the drawn ghost
stood still for 50–80 ms and then jumped 48 u — next to other ghosts.

**b) Frame pacing** — `requestAnimationFrame` intervals of the player client in headless
Chromium at 1280×800, 60 s of straight runs:

| Run                              | Frames | > 25 ms      | > 40 ms   | Longest | Machine's CPU load |
| -------------------------------- | ------ | ------------ | --------- | ------- | ------------------ |
| client                           | 3275   | 333 (10 %)   | 5         | 200 ms  | 36–43 %            |
| client (later)                   | 2452   | 1096 (45 %)  | 88        | 667 ms  | 100 %              |
| empty page                       | 3639   | 6 (0.2 %)    | 0         | 33 ms   | 43 → 100 %         |

The client's own work per frame (its `requestAnimationFrame` callback): p50 2.7 ms, p95
5.3 ms, 6 of 2464 over 12 ms. The long frames are the headless browser's software raster of
the canvas under the machine's load, not the game's script: nothing changed there.

**c) Arrival** — the server states' intervals at the client (tick 50 ms), N 1198 per run, four
runs: mean 50 ms, σ 5.7–6.7 ms, p95 59–62 ms, the longest 67–85 ms, at most two over 70 ms.
Even; and the own ghost is drawn from the prediction only, never from the state buffer.

**The cause and the fix.** The largest cause was the prediction (a): its path stopped at the
other ghosts where they are drawn — a render buffer late — while the server pushes them aside,
so the path fell behind and the correction pulled it on in jerks. Now the path runs as the
server's ghost that touches nobody; what is drawn follows it by the path's own motion and closes
any gap within 100 ms, at most at running speed on top — no jump — and still never through a
drawn ghost (it stops at one from its side and slides round it). Away from other ghosts the
uneven frames went from 61 to 14 (Sumo) and 60 to 14 (Bomb); next to them the server's own
pushes still change the speed, which the client cannot foresee. Runs differ in how much time
the scripted player spends straight and near others (bots, falls, a match end).
