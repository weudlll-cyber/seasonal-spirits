The jump criterion is a strict `0/60s`. Run 3 sees 32 events across 32 ghosts × 60 fps ×
60 s = 115 200 observations, i.e. 0.028 % of samples. Each event is at most a single-frame
overshoot by a few units past the jump detector's threshold of
`2 × speed × frameDt + 2 ≈ 12 units` — likely imperceptible to a human. Three questions
for the owner:

1. **Accept and continue** (declare Phase 0 cleared, start Piece 3)? The two numerical
   criteria PASS cleanly on this box; the third criterion is strict interpretation of a
   subjective condition.
2. **Change technology** (WebRTC, server push over SSE, different serialisation)?
3. **Change the concept** (lower tick rate, bigger threshold, different latency band)?
